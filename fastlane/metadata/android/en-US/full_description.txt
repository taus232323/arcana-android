Arcana is a modern secure messenger for private conversations with friends, family, and teams.

Sign up with your email, chat in personal and group rooms, share media, and make voice and video calls — with end-to-end encryption by default.

<b>What you can do</b>
 • Instant messaging in 1:1 and group chats
 • Voice and video calls
 • Photos, files, voice messages, and links
 • Reactions, replies, polls, and pinned messages
 • Invite people with a simple Arcana invite link
 • Stay in sync across devices

<b>Built for privacy</b>
Your conversations are end-to-end encrypted. After you confirm your email, your device is trusted for encrypted messaging — no complicated setup.

We don’t mine your chats for ads. You’re here to communicate, not to become the product.

<b>Simple to start</b>
Create an account with email, confirm it, and you’re ready. No phone number required.
